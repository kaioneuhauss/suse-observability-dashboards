Content publication uses finite Helm hook Jobs.
  kubectl logs job/{{ .Release.Name }}-dashboards -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  kubectl logs job/{{ .Release.Name }}-monitors -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
Use the explicit kubeconfig and context for your target cluster.
Jobs must complete and report verified persisted content. A successful render alone is insufficient.
Uninstall removes Kubernetes resources; SUSE objects are retained unless explicitly removed through the guarded lifecycle.
